Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Get Gorgeous Salon & Spa (“we,” “our”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as:

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you contact us or book an appointment
  • Service preferences and appointment history
  • Messages you send through our contact form or email

When you visit our website, we or our hosting and analytics providers may automatically collect certain information (e.g., IP address, browser type, pages visited) for operation and improvement of the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, schedule, and manage appointments and services
  • Respond to your inquiries and communicate with you
  • Send appointment reminders or relevant updates if you have agreed to them
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with applicable law

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who assist us (e.g., booking platforms, email delivery) under agreements that protect your data. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time. You can request access to or correction of your personal information by contacting us. Where applicable law grants you rights to deletion or portability, we will honor them.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality and analytics. You can adjust your browser settings to limit or block cookies.

7. Children

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of our website or services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at getgorgeousmn@gmail.com or (651) 291-8997.
