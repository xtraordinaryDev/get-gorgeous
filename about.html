About Get Gorgeous

We are a Minnesota-based salon dedicated to healthy, beautiful hair for women of color. Our team combines expert technique with a deep understanding of textured hair to create styles that celebrate your natural beauty.

From protective styles to silk presses and nourishing treatments, every service is tailored to you. At Get Gorgeous, we believe that your hair is your crown, and we're here to help you wear it with confidence and pride.

Our salon provides a welcoming, professional environment where you can relax, rejuvenate, and leave feeling absolutely gorgeous. We specialize in all hair textures and types, offering everything from natural hair care to extensions, braids, and elegant styling for special occasions.

MEET THE TEAM

Ilka Bird

Owner / Stylist

At the age of 16, I realized I had passion for bringing the natural beauty out of people. I am a Brooklyn, New York native. I grew up around style, beauty and glamour! So I try to give that look to all my clients of all ages. In June 2007, I opened Get Gorgeous Salon. The salon is located in the Historic Rondo Neighborhood of St. Paul. At Get Gorgeous, we pride ourselves on being a multicultural salon with family love vibes. We try to make all feel welcome with a range of ages that come to Get Gorgeous!

Deanna

Manager

Hello, my name is Deanna! As I am a native of the Rondo area, I am deeply committed to supporting my community. I bring a wealth of experience in customer service, ensuring that each client receives exceptional care and attention. My passion for helping others drives me to create a welcoming environment at Get Gorgeous Salon. I believe in building lasting relationships with clients and enhancing their overall experience. My dedication to our community and clients is at the heart of everything I do.

Justina

Braiding Specialist

Hello! My name is Justina. I am a North Carolina native and Georgia-raised woman from the south who moved to Minnesota in 2024. Since the age of 18, I have dedicated myself to the art of braiding hair and I am excited to share my expertise with the clients at Get Gorgeous Salon. I believe in the power of beauty to enhance confidence and self-expression, and I am committed to providing a transformative experience for everyone I work with. Hope to see you soon in my chair!

Tasha P

Stylist

Meet Tasha P. A native to the Twin Cities and a traveler of the world, she loves all things beautiful and delicious. She is a firm believer that feeling beautiful is a necessity of life—yet it’s a privilege that not all women get to experience. So that’s what she wants: to give you an experience in feeling beautiful. Custom cuts and color are her jam—styling, updos, extensions, relaxers, perms, keratin treatments, and more. When not behind the chair, she is a member of the 3x Grammy award-winning group Sounds of Blackness and wears many hats. Don’t be surprised if she busts out in song or is out of the salon performing a gig, real quick!

LaLa B

Braider

LaLa is an experienced braider from St. Paul, with a profound passion for beauty that began at the age of 12. Her journey started with learning to style her own hair, which ignited her interest in the art of braiding. Over the years, LaLa has honed her craft—transforming clients’ looks and boosting their confidence with each braided style. She finds immense fulfillment in witnessing the positive impact her work has on others, as they leave her chair. LaLa’s dedication to her art continues through makeup and nails as she evolves in the beauty industry. And we are happy to have her on our team!
