Meet Our Stylists

Textured-hair specialists who blend healthy hair care, artistry, and a welcoming experience.

Ilka Bird

Owner / Stylist

Brooklyn-born and style-obsessed, Ilka opened Get Gorgeous in 2007 to create a multicultural salon with family-love vibes. She delights in bringing out natural beauty for clients of all ages in St. Paul’s Historic Rondo neighborhood.

Braiding Specialist

A North Carolina native raised in Georgia and now in Minnesota, Justina has honed the art of braiding since 18. She blends precision technique with a passion for confidence-building, crafting transformative chair experiences.
